# The website: the owner's brief (2026-10-19)

From the owner's voice notes and references. Not started. There is no marketing site in this
repository yet; `apps/web` is the app itself.

## What he wants
- **A landing page for محرر زود PDF** that shows, rather than tells. Where the current pages show
  still pictures and icons, it should play short videos and animation.
- **The opening (hero)**: papers scattered all around, which gather, straighten and stack into
  one organized file, ending on the ZOOD logo for the PDF editor. One seamless motion.
- **The ZOOD pattern, animated**, like paint dripping from one page down to the next (the same
  pattern the Zood theme uses: `apps/ios/PDFEditor/Design/`, `docs/design/`). He notes the
  drip should not run at one even speed everywhere.
- **Then the features**, each shown moving, as in his references: the AI assistant, ease of use,
  the ring (quick tools), the phone, organizing pages and documents, and the rest.
- **Copy that sells outcomes, not features.** His example: not "smart accounting software for
  modern businesses" but "close your books in minutes, not hours". Every headline should say
  what the person gets. Arabic first.
- **The phone version must be compact and beautiful**: he finds the current mobile layout loose.

## His references (local only, git-ignored: `docs/research/website-refs/`)
Third-party work, for direction only: never copied or published.
- `vdas-1..4.mp4` (Tiimo UI/UX motion): soft 3D, lilac/glass, task cards floating and
  settling into order, a tablet on a desk with cards around it. **The look for the hero.**
- `kabarza-bookshelf.mp4`: a scroll-driven page where books slide off a shelf and open.
- `snows-aqua.mp4`: large type over soft gradients, a hero that moves as you scroll.
- `madd-archive-folders.jpg` (مَدّ): stacked folder tabs, each holding a site — the "archive
  folders" idea for showing the tools.
- `*.sheet.jpg`: six frames of each video, to look at without playing it.
