## English (en-US)
- App name (30): ZOOD PDF: Arabic PDF Editor
- Short description (80): Read, sign and edit PDFs in Arabic. Your files stay on your phone.
- Full description:
A PDF editor built for Arabic from the first line. Text stays joined and right to left, and search finds a word with or without its tashkeel.

Read: open any PDF from Files, Gmail or WhatsApp, with night mode and read aloud.
Sign and fill: draw your signature, fill forms, add the date and a stamp.
Edit and organise: mark up and highlight, edit text, reorder pages, combine and split files, compress, add a password, or redact what nobody should see.
Convert: to Word, Excel and pictures, pictures to PDF, and Arabic text recognition in scanned files.
Ask ZOOD: summaries, explanations and translation on the phone itself, once its model is downloaded. No account, no key, no uploads.
Control your computer: scan the code in ZOOD PDF on your PC to present, move the pointer and send files between them.

No account, no ads, no tracking. A file leaves your phone only when you share it.
