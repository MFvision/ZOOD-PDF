Getting started

Open a PDF

Three ways to open a file. It never leaves your device.

  1. On the home screen, press Open or create, then Open PDF….
  2. Or drag the file onto the app window and drop it.
  3. Or open a recent file from Recents with one click.
  • Shortcut: Ctrl+O (⌘O on a Mac).

PDF guide

Save your changes

The original file stays untouched until you save.

  1. Press Save in the top bar, or Ctrl+S (⌘S on a Mac).
  2. On the computer it saves into the same file; in a browser it downloads a new copy.
  • Every saved version is kept under Versions, so you can go back.

PDF guide

Change the language and the look

Arabic or English, light or dark, and several looks.

  1. Open Settings › General to change the language.
  2. Open Settings › Appearance for the colours, the look and the icon size.

PDF guide

Edit and comment

Edit the text in a PDF

Change words where they are, with Arabic kept correct.

  1. Open the file and press Edit text in the top bar.
  2. Click the paragraph you want and type. The lines reflow by themselves.
  3. To undo: Ctrl+Z. Then save.
  • To add a new line anywhere, use the Edit tool.

PDF guide

Highlight and add notes

Highlight, underline, add sticky notes and draw on the page.

  1. Open the Comment tool.
  2. Choose Highlight, then select the text with the mouse.
  3. For a note, choose Sticky note and click where it goes.

PDF guide

Pages and files

Rotate, delete and reorder pages

All the pages in front of you: drag to reorder.

  1. Open the Organize pages tool.
  2. Drag a page to its new place.
  3. Select a page and use the buttons: rotate, delete, extract, insert.

PDF guide

Combine several files into one

PDFs, pictures and Office files into one PDF.

  1. Open the Combine tool.
  2. Add the files and drag them into order.
  3. Press Combine and save the new file.
  • On the computer: select the files in a folder, right-click and choose Combine, without opening the app.

PDF guide

Make a file smaller

Compress it so it goes by e-mail or WhatsApp easily.

  1. Open the Compress tool.
  2. Choose how strong, then press Compress.
  3. You see the size before and after. Save the result.

PDF guide

Compare two versions

Shows what was added and removed, word by word.

  1. Open the first version and open Compare.
  2. Choose the other one: another file or an earlier saved version.

PDF guide

Search across all your files

The Library searches every file you added, not just the open one.

  1. Open the Library tool and add a folder.
  2. Type a word: the files and pages with it appear.

PDF guide

Fill and sign

Fill in a form and sign it

Type into the fields and sign by hand or with a picture of your signature.

  1. Open the Fill & sign tool.
  2. Click any field and type.
  3. Press Create a signature: draw it, type it or pick a picture, then place it on the page.
  • Your details (name, address, bank) in Settings › My details fill forms in one click, and stay on your device.

PDF guide

Convert and create

Read a scanned file (OCR)

Turn page pictures into text you can search and copy, in Arabic and English.

  1. Open the scanned file and open Scan & OCR.
  2. Choose the language and start.
  3. When it finishes, the text can be searched and copied. Save the file.

PDF guide

Turn a PDF into Word, Excel or PowerPoint

Export the text, tables and slides, with Arabic right to left.

  1. Open the Export tool.
  2. Choose the kind: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, pictures or text.
  3. Press Export and save the file.
  • Word export carries the text as flowing paragraphs, not the exact page layout.

PDF guide

Make a PDF from Word or pictures

Word, Excel, PowerPoint and pictures to PDF.

  1. On the home screen, press Open or create, then Create PDF….
  2. Pick the file or the pictures.
  3. Check the result and save it.

Protect and privacy

Put a password on a file

Only someone with the password can open it.

  1. Open the Protect tool.
  2. Press Set a password and type it twice.
  3. Save the file. Keep the password: it cannot be recovered.

PDF guide

Remove private details for good

Redaction really removes the text, it does not just cover it.

  1. Open the Redact tool.
  2. Mark the text or an area, or search for a word to mark it on every page.
  3. Press Apply redactions, then save.

AI

Listen to a file

The app reads the page to you in an Arabic or English voice.

  1. Open the file and press Read aloud in the top bar.
  2. Choose: this page, from here to the end, or the selection.
  3. Change the speed and the voice in the same bar.

PDF guide

Ask the AI about your file

Summarize, ask and translate. It runs on your device, offline, once downloaded.

  1. Open the AI tool.
  2. The first time: download the on-device model in Settings › AI.
  3. Type your question. The answer names the page it came from.

PDF guide

Ask ZOOD in any app Desktop

One key: speak or type a request, in any app.

  1. Hold Ctrl+Shift+Space (⌘⇧Space on a Mac) and speak.
  2. Or tap once to type a request, or twice in a reply box and it writes the reply.
  3. Change the keys in Settings › AI.
  • On a Mac it needs Input Monitoring, Accessibility and the microphone.

On your computer

Quick tools without opening the app Desktop

Right-click or the ring: compress, combine, convert straight away.

  1. Select one or more files in a folder, right-click, then choose the tool.
  2. Or drag the files with Ctrl held to bring up the ring, and drop them on a tool.
  3. Arrange the tools in Settings › Quick tools.

Control it from your phone Desktop

Present, move the pointer and send files from your phone.

  1. In the Tools menu, choose Phone remote….
  2. Scan the code with the phone camera. Both must be on the same network.

Make ZOOD PDF the default for PDFs Desktop

So PDFs open in it with a double-click.

  1. Windows: Settings › General › Open Default apps, then choose ZOOD PDF for .pdf.
  2. Mac: select a PDF in Finder, File › Get Info, choose ZOOD PDF under Open with, then Change All.

Updates arrive by themselves, or ask for one now.

Getting help

Report a problem or suggest an idea

No account: write the problem and follow its status, or vote for ideas.

  1. Open Help › Help & Support, then Report a problem.
  2. Write what happened. You see everything that will be sent and choose whether to attach the log and a picture.
  3. Its status shows in the same list: open, in progress, fixed.
  4. In Ideas, write your own idea or vote for others.

Common questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your device. Nothing leaves it unless you choose to send a report or an idea.

Do I need an account or a subscription?

No. The app works with no account, support and voting included.

Does it work offline?

Yes, all of it, the AI too once its model is downloaded once.

Where are my signatures and details kept?

On this device only, in the system’s secure store for your details.

I cannot select or search the text

The file is most likely scanned pictures. Run Scan & OCR first.

Known limits

  • Word export carries the text as flowing paragraphs, not the exact page layout.
  • Printing on Windows sends the pages as sharp pictures (300 dpi), not as vector text.
  • On a Mac, Ask ZOOD does not work outside the app until Input Monitoring, Accessibility and the microphone are allowed.

Didn’t find it? In the app: Help › Report a problem. No account, and you follow your report until it is fixed.
